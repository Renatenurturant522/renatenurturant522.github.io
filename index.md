## 🛠 What this tool does

This application functions as a gateway. When you send a request to use an artificial intelligence model, Switchboard looks at your available providers. It chooses the most reliable option based on your preferences. If your primary choice fails, this tool switches to a backup automatically.

The application works locally on your machine. It keeps a record of every routing decision inside a database file on your computer. You can review these logs to see how the software handles your requests. This transparency helps you understand your usage patterns and costs.

## ⚙️ Configuring your providers

When you open Switchboard for the first time, you must enter your credentials for the services you wish to use. The program supports sixteen different providers, including major names in artificial intelligence.

1. Open the application interface.
2. Select the Settings menu from the sidebar.
3. Click on the Providers tab.
4. Choose a service from the list.
5. Paste your private API key into the designated field.
6. Click the Save button to store the key securely in your local database.

Repeat these steps for every service you want to include in your routing setup. If you do not have a key for a specific provider, leave the box blank.

## 🚦 Understanding smart routing

Switchboard uses a logic system to determine which provider handles your work. You can set rules for this process in the Routing tab. 

*   Cost optimization: The software tracks current prices and picks the cheaper service.
*   Speed priority: The software monitors response times and picks the fastest provider.
*   Reliability mode: The software checks the status of services and avoids ones that report errors.

The application automatically logs these decisions. If you want to see why the software chose a specific path, look at the Activity log at the bottom of the main screen.

## 🔐 Privacy and security

This software runs locally on your machine. It does not send your data to any central server owned by the developers. Your keys remain inside the database file on your hard drive. 

*   The local database uses standard encryption formats.
*   All communications remain between your app and the provider you choose.
*   You control which providers receive your requests.

## 🔧 Frequently asked questions

**Can I run this without an internet connection?**
No. You need an active connection because the software sends requests to external artificial intelligence services.

**What happens if all my providers go offline?**
The software will report a connection error in your activity log. You should check your network status or verify that your provider service is operational.

**How do I update the software?**
When a new version exists, a notification appears on the home screen. Click the update link to download the latest installer, which will automatically replace the older version.

**Does this software store my chat history?**
Switchboard tracks routing metadata and technical details about the connection. It does not store the content of your conversations for long-term tracking unless you enable specific debugging features.

**Is my API key safe?**
Yes. Your keys are stored locally on your own computer. The software does not share them or send them to third-party servers.
