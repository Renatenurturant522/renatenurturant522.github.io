How Website Support Works

BrightPath Web Design provides maintenance and support when requested by the client. To receive help, clients must notify us when they notice a problem, need an update, or experience an issue with their website.

  • Clients are responsible for bringing website problems to our attention.
  • We do not provide continuous 24/7 monitoring or automatic problem detection.
  • Work begins after we receive and review a support request.
  • Response and completion times depend on the issue and our agreed service schedule.
  • Work beyond the included monthly time or plan scope may require an additional quote.

Specific services, response expectations, billing terms, and exclusions will be confirmed in the client's maintenance agreement before work begins.
